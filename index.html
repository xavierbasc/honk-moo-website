Honk & Moo

Tap and listen!

Eleven little worlds full of animals, vehicles and things that make a sound. Tap one and it moves, sounds and spells its name.

Where to play What is it?

Ages 1–4 · iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows, Linux and Android · $2.99, one payment

How it works

Pick a world, tap any card and watch it hop, swim, drive, fly or wobble while it sounds and spells its name. Swipe or use the arrows to change world.

What's inside

Eleven worlds, 88 friends

Farm, jungle, ocean, road, sky, music, bugs, food, dinosaurs, home and weather — plus a surprise world that mixes everything.

A sound for every one

Moo, honk, buzz, roar, kettle whistle… all synthesised, never harsh.

First words

Tap a card and its English name drops in, letter by letter, on wooden blocks.

Nothing to get wrong

No score, no timer, no losing: just tap and listen.

For grown-ups

Honk & Moo is made to be handed to a small child without worrying:

  • No ads and no in-app purchases. One payment, that's it.
  • No data collection. The game has no accounts, no analytics and no network code at all.
  • No links out. Settings and the quit button sit behind a button you must hold for two seconds.
  • Works offline, on the plane and in the car.
  • Gentle sound: music sits low under the effects, and both have their own volume.
